Password Security Tips

Follow these best practices to keep your accounts safe and secure.

Use Strong Passwords

Create passwords with at least 8 characters including letters, numbers and symbols.

Use Unique Passwords

Never use the same password on multiple websites or accounts.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security with 2FA whenever possible.

Change Passwords Regularly

Change your passwords periodically, especially for important accounts.

Beware of Phishing

Don't click on suspicious links or share your password in emails.

Keep Your Devices Secure

Lock your devices and keep your software up to date.

⚠

IMPORTANT:

Never share your password with anyone.

Avoid using the same password on multiple websites.
